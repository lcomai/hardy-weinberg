Need a formula reminder?
p + q = 1
p = (2 × AA count + Aa count) / (2 × population size)
Expected AA = p²; Aa = 2pq; aa = q².
If the fully recessive phenotype frequency is given and Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium is assumed, q = √(recessive phenotype frequency).
